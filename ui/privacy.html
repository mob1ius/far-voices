Privacy

The short version, which is the whole version

Nothing is stored on your device. No cookies, no local storage, no identifiers of any kind. This site cannot recognise you tomorrow and does not want to.

Two things are recorded

A request log. Your IP address, your network and country, which page you asked for, what the server said back, your browser's User-Agent, and the site you came from with the query string cut off before anything is written. It exists to tell visitors apart from scanners, which at present is most of the traffic. Kept 30 days, then deleted.

A usage summary. Counts of which switches got flipped and how long the visit lasted, sent once as you leave. No address, no identifier, nothing you typed. Kept indefinitely, because there is nothing in it to protect.

The two cannot be joined. One knows where you came from and nothing about what you did. The other knows what you did and nothing about where you came from. That is structural, not a policy.

Turning it off

Send Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track and no summary is sent at all. The deck behaves identically. It was never the interesting part.

The legal bit

Lawful basis for the request log is legitimate interest in network and information security, UK and EU GDPR Recital 49. The usage summary has no identifier in it, so it is not personal data and needs no basis. Nothing from either is sold, shared, or handed to anybody.

Asking

farvoices@proton.me will get you the full technical description in writing, a copy of anything the log holds about you, or its deletion. Complaints can also go to your national data protection authority.
